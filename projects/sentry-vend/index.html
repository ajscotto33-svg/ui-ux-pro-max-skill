How It Works

Four steps. No staff involved.

Every transaction runs the same sequence. If any step fails, the machine does not dispense and the attempt is written to the audit log.

01

Scan The ID

The reader lifts the PDF417 barcode off a driver’s license or state ID and reads the date of birth directly.

02

Confirm The Face

Facial age estimation compares the person at the machine to the credential. A mismatch stops the sale.

03

Select The Product

Live inventory on a 15-inch touchscreen. Empty slots are hidden, so nobody pays for a jam.

04

Dispense And Log

The unit dispenses, writes a verification record against the sale, and updates stock counts in real time.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Still have questions? Send them with your placement request and we will answer before anything is installed.

Two checks, in sequence. The scanner reads the PDF417 barcode on the back of a government-issued ID and pulls the date of birth and expiration date. Then facial age estimation compares the person standing at the machine to the credential. Both have to pass. Expired IDs, damaged barcodes, and mismatches are all refusals.

The machine refuses and dispenses nothing. The attempt is timestamped and written to the audit log with the refusal reason. Repeated attempts at one unit trigger an alert to our operations team and to the venue contact. No payment is captured on a failed verification.

Door sensors and an accelerometer fire an alert the moment the cabinet is forced or moved. The unit locks itself, stops dispensing, and pushes the event over LTE. We can also lock any machine in the fleet remotely from a laptop. The cabinet is bolted to the floor or wall at install, and the hardware is insured by us, not by you.

You take 20% of net sales from your unit, paid monthly by ACH with a per-transaction statement. There is no upfront cost, no lease, no equipment rental, and no minimum volume clause. We carry the cost of the machine, the stock, the connectivity, and the service route.

Weekly on a fixed route, plus an off-cycle visit whenever telemetry shows a selection below 20% stock. Restocking takes about fifteen minutes and we schedule it outside your peak hours. Your staff never handles product and never counts inventory.

In most jurisdictions, yes — the sale happens on your premises, so the retail permit is tied to your address. We check your state and county requirements during the placement review and hand you the exact filing list before install. Federal rules also restrict vending machine sales to facilities where people under 21 are not permitted at any time, which is why we place only in 21+ venues.

Under two hours. We need a 39-inch-wide clear span against a wall, one standard 110V outlet, and access to move a 640-pound cabinet through your doors. No plumbing, no network drop, no electrical work. We anchor it, stock it, run three test transactions with your manager, and leave.

The agreement runs twelve months with a 30-day exit for any reason. We pull the unit, patch the anchor points, and settle your final revenue share on the next cycle. If a placement is not earning, we would rather move it than argue about it.
