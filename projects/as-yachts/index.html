Buying with AS Yachts

A considered path to ownership.

From the first brief to the moment the flag is raised, one broker guides you through every stage.

  1. 01

    The Search

    We translate your brief — size, style, cruising grounds — into a shortlist that includes off-market yachts unavailable elsewhere.

  2. 02

    Private Viewings

    Discreet inspections arranged worldwide, on your schedule, with full technical briefings from our in-house surveyors.

  3. 03

    Negotiation

    We structure and lead the offer, protecting your position and your privacy from letter of intent to signed MOA.

  4. 04

    Survey & Sea Trial

    Independent condition and sea trials coordinated with class societies to confirm every system before you commit.

  5. 05

    Legal & Flag

    Escrow, registration, VAT and flag advisory handled with maritime counsel — nothing left to chance.

  6. 06

    Closing & Handover

    Funds released, title transferred, and a seamless handover to your captain, crew and management team.
