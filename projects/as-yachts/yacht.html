The Yacht

A study in light & volume.

Photographed across three days in the Mediterranean. Full high-resolution set available on request.

Exterior

Decks made for gathering

Four exterior decks flow from a beach club at the waterline to a private sun deck crowned with a glass-edge jacuzzi. Teak underfoot, brushed-steel rails, and shade that moves with the sun.

  • Foredeck jacuzzi with surrounding sun pads
  • Fold-out beach club with sea terraces
  • Al-fresco dining for 14 on the main aft deck
  • Sky lounge bar & 180° sun deck
  • Shaded lounge with retractable awnings

Interior

Interiors by a master atelier

Full-height glass dissolves the line between salon and sea. Book-matched marble, pale oak and hand-loomed textiles run through six staterooms and a main-deck owner's apartment.

  • Full-beam owner's suite with private terrace
  • Five further guest cabins, all en-suite
  • Formal & informal dining salons
  • Cinema lounge and library
  • Spa with hammam, sauna and treatment room

The Builder

Benetti

Founded in 1873, Benetti is among the most storied names in yachting — a builder synonymous with displacement superyachts of exceptional volume and craft.

Aurelia represents the yard's flagship displacement platform, engineered for genuine ocean-crossing range and finished to the standard expected of a fully custom build. Delivered in 2021 and meticulously maintained under a single ownership, with a full cosmetic refit completed in 2024.
