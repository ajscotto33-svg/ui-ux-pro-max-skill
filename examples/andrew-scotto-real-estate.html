About Andrew

A Trusted Name in Luxury Real Estate

Andrew Scotto has spent over fifteen years navigating Manhattan's most competitive listings and Miami's ultra-luxury waterfront market, closing more than $1.2 billion in residential real estate.

His approach pairs old-world discretion with data-driven market intelligence — giving buyers and sellers a decisive edge in negotiations that matter. Clients return to Andrew not for the transaction, but for the outcome.

Licensed in both New York and Florida, Andrew leads a boutique practice built on referrals, repeat clients, and an unwavering standard of service.
